Print and illustration · 2018–2025

Christmas card range

A range of original illustrated greeting cards and prints celebrating St Albans, sold through local stockists and direct to customers.

My role
Everything: illustration, print-ready artwork, print production, pricing, stockist relationships, marketing and sales.
Tools
Watercolour, ink and Adobe Photoshop; litho and digital print production.
Outcome
Chosen by the Mayor of St Albans for the official Christmas card (2018). The doorway design won the St Albans Art Society Trophy (2022). Stocked by St Albans Museum + Gallery for five years and by the Cathedral gift shop, and licensed to the Addison’s Disease Self-Help Group for fundraising cards.
