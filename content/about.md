Designer and illustrator who crafts beautiful things by hand and engineers the tools to make more of them.

Seven years running my own illustration and greeting card business taught me the unglamorous half: print-ready files, pricing, stockists, shop shelves. A BA in Computer Animation and an MSc in Computer Science gave me the other: generative pattern systems, interactive apps, and experience directing films and leading game projects.

I care about work that holds up in use: images that tell the story in a single frame, and systems that make sense to the people they’re for.
