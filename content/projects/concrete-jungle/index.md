intro: >-
  CJ is a character from Concrete Jungle, a third-year film by my course-mate Oisin Fitzpatrick, made in the same final-project module as my own Capturing the Sun. Oisin developed the story and the concept; I built CJ in 3D.

  Before sculpting, I asked Oisin about CJ’s build and how he would move. He wanted a strong boxer’s build, so I built my own reference board on top of what he had provided, including boxers’ and labourers’ hands. I put the most detail where the storyboards showed the camera would be: the face, hands, torso and shoes, with a silhouette and clothes that let his physique read.

  I sculpted the body in ZBrush, modelled the clothes in Maya and refined them in ZBrush, then retopologised the whole character in Maya. The hands were going to be animated in close-up and the shoes would come near the camera, so I built the topology for detailed finger movement and a clean foot roll, and chose a pose that suited his range of motion. Others in the team took him on through UVs, textures, rigging and animation.
