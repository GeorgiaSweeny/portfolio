intro: >-
  Two head sculpts from 2023, both made in ZBrush.

  Anton Ego, the food critic from Pixar’s Ratatouille, began as a speed sculpt in my second-year 3D sculpture unit. We then had to take one of our speed sculpts to a polished concept sculpt, ready to texture. I chose Ego, refined him, retopologised him with ZBrush’s masking tools and unwrapped his UVs.

  The portrait is of a friend who wanted to 3D print their head as a bust, so I sculpted their likeness as a gift: just the head, no hair.
