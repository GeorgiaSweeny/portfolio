intro: >-
  Two proud philosophers, a wizened old woman and an impossible task: to capture a piece of the sun in three days. One hunts for riches, the other for a clever trick, but neither proves as wise as he claims.

  Inspired by Paulo Coelho’s The Alchemist, it started out as a pitch for my group’s shadow play in my first year and came back in my third year as my final major project, growing into a full storyboarded animatic with its characters developed further.

  Its world then took shape in 3D as a previs (a rough 3D version used to plan the film’s shots): three environments in Unreal Engine, modelled, textured and lit. The story is still in development as a side project.
