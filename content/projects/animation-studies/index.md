intro: >-
  Three short loops, each practising a different side of animation: timing and weight in 2D with a hamster running on its wheel, body mechanics in 3D with a sneak, and placing a 3D object convincingly into live-action footage with a simulated can.

  The hamster wheel is a 2D loop, animated frame by frame in Procreate. The sneak is from my Animation Production unit at university: a body mechanics shot, animated from reference footage we filmed of ourselves. The brief allowed a walk, run, skip or similar, and focused on weight, balance, the line of action (the curve that runs through a pose) and working pose to pose (key poses first, then the frames between).

  The can is from Tools for Production, a first-year unit. I chose a backplate (the filmed shot the can would sit in), prepared dummy geometry matching the scene for the can to land on, then modelled the can, unwrapped its UVs and textured it in Photoshop, the method the unit set out to teach. Finally I lit it to match the footage and simulated it falling into place, all in Maya.
